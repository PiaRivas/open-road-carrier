Frequently asked questions

Why is now a good time to invest in trucking?

Trade policy, tariffs, and a broader push to bring manufacturing back to the U.S. are reshaping supply chains in real time. Whatever the political outcome, one reality doesn't change: goods still have to move, and in the U.S. that overwhelmingly means by truck. As freight volumes grow and per-mile rates climb, that upside flows directly to the carriers and equipment already on the road — not to a policy bet or a ticker symbol. It's also a chance to put your capital somewhere you can point to and say what it's doing — not just what it might return.

How will I know how my investment is performing?

You get two layers of visibility: real-time GPS tracking gives you 24/7 insight into where your truck is and whether it's on the road, and we send detailed quarterly reports covering revenue, expenses, maintenance, and your payout — whether that's your share of profit or your fixed return.

What's the difference between the two investment formats?

With the Profit-Sharing Partnership, you fund the truck and split 50% of the net profit it generates — your upside grows with performance. With the Fixed Annual Return, you earn a flat 20% per year on a regular schedule, regardless of month-to-month swings in freight rates or fuel costs.

What happens if a truck needs repairs or is out of service?

Our operations team handles maintenance and repairs directly, and every truck carries physical damage insurance covering roughly 50% of the total investment — helping minimize downside risk and keep downtime and losses to a minimum.

How much do I need to invest to get started?

Investment size is flexible — from funding a single truck to backing a small dedicated fleet. Reach out and we'll walk through what fits your goals.
